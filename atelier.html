01 — Why we made it

The things we couldn't find.

So we built them ourselves — in our Saigon lab, with our chemists, in batches small enough that we know every drop. Hasaki Atelier is what happens when a retailer stops complaining and starts making.

03 — How it's made

From brief to bottle.

Step 01 — Brief

We notice a gap.

Our buyers track what sells, what doesn't, what customers ask for. When the same gap shows up three times, we start a formula brief.

Step 02 — Formulate

12–18 months of R&D.

Our in-house chemists run 40–60 iterations per formula. Then 6 weeks of in-vitro testing, then 12 weeks of human panel testing in three climates.

Step 03 — Small batch

Made in Saigon.

Batches of 3,000 units max — small enough that every batch gets QA'd by hand. Cruelty-free. Tested in three climates before going live.

Step 04 — Honest price

No retailer markup.

Because we sell direct, you pay the cost of the formula + 30% — not 4× like a typical luxury brand. That's how a $32 Vitamin C exists.
