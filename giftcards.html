Common questions.

Do gift cards expire?+

No. Hasaki gift cards never expire — unused balance stays on the card forever.

When will the recipient get it?+

If you choose "Send right now," they'll receive an email within a few minutes of your order being placed. If you schedule, it'll deliver at 9am their local time on the date you choose.

Can it be used in combination with promo codes?+

Yes. Gift cards work like a payment method — you can stack them with promo codes and member rewards.

Refund policy on gift cards?+

Gift cards are non-refundable once delivered. If a gift card hasn't been delivered yet, you can cancel within 30 minutes of placing the order.
