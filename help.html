Help center

How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about orders, shipping, returns and your Hasaki Member account.

Shipping & delivery

Is shipping really free over $35?+

Yes. Standard shipping is free on every order $35 or more (after discounts). Under $35, standard shipping is $5.99.

How fast is delivery?+

Standard delivery is 3–5 business days. Express delivery is 1–2 business days for $8. In LA, Hồ Chí Minh and Hà Nội we offer 2-hour delivery for $14 — order by 6pm.

Do you ship internationally?+

Yes — we ship to 30+ countries. International rates calculated at checkout based on destination.

Returns & refunds

Can I return an opened product?+

Yes. We accept returns on opened products within 30 days of delivery, no questions asked. We pay for return shipping.

How long do refunds take?+

Refunds are processed within 3 business days of receiving your return and appear on your card within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

How do I start a return?+

Sign in to your account, go to Orders, and click "Start a return" next to the order. We'll email you a prepaid label.

Orders

Where is my order?+

You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. You can also track your order here at any time with your order number.

Can I change or cancel an order?+

You can cancel or modify your order within 30 minutes of placing it from your Order page. After that, it's already in our fulfilment center.

Where are my free samples?+

Every order comes with 3 free samples — you choose them at checkout. Pink Gold members get 5; Pink Diamond members get unlimited.

Hasaki Member & account

How do I earn points?+

Earn 1 point per $1 spent. Tier multipliers apply: Pink Gold gets 1.6×, Pink Diamond gets 2×. See the full Rewards page →

How do I redeem my points?+

100 points = $5 off. Apply at checkout under "Use my points". You can stack points with promo codes.

I forgot my password+

Click "Sign in" then "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link.
