# Product images

Drop real product photos in this folder, then tell Claude the filename and product name and they will be wired into the homepage.

## Recommended specs

- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Dimensions:** 600 × 750 pixels (4:5 aspect, matches the bestseller card)
- **Background:** white, off-white, or transparent (PNG) — Sephora/Rhode style
- **File size:** under 200 KB after compression
- **Naming:** `brand-product-shortname.jpg` — e.g. `laneige-lip-sleeping-mask.jpg`, `ysl-libre-edp.jpg`

- Brand press kits / brand portals (the licensed/highest-quality option)
