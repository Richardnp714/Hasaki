How does the 14-day free trial work?

Start with full Pro access — no credit card needed. At the end of 14 days, choose a plan or stay on the free tier. We'll never charge you without warning.

Can I migrate from another platform?

Yes. We support one-click imports from Vercel, Railway, Render, Heroku, and AWS. Our team also offers free white-glove migration assistance for Enterprise customers.

Is my data secure?

NovaSaaS is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We never sell or share your data.

What support is available?

Free plan includes community support. Pro includes email + chat with a 4-hour response SLA. Enterprise gets a dedicated success manager and 1-hour SLA 24/7.
