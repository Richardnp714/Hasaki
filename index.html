07 — About hasaki.

From Saigon
to Los Angeles.

Hasaki was founded in 2016 in Ho Chi Minh City with a single mission: Real Quality, Real Value. By marrying authentic global brands with honest pricing, we redefined how Vietnam shops for beauty — and became the country's #1 beauty chain in less than a decade.

Backed by Alibaba and growing 39% year-over-year, we now serve 12 million+ customers across 300+ stores, with 3 million monthly active app users. In April 2026, we opened our first US flagship in Los Angeles — the start of a 200-store American footprint by 2030.

"Save More Always isn't a tagline. It's a contract with every customer who walks through our doors."
