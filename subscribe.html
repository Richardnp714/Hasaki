Common questions.

Can I change my box later?+

Yes — swap, pause, or cancel from your account dashboard any time before your next ship date.

What's in each box?+

Our beauty team curates each box based on the season, your skin profile (built from the skin quiz), and the latest cult launches. You'll preview the contents 7 days before shipping and can swap individual items.

When am I charged?+

First box is charged today and ships within 48 hours. Future boxes are charged 24 hours before the ship date — you'll get an email reminder.

Do points still apply?+

Yes — every subscription order earns Hasaki Member points at your current tier. Subscriptions also qualify you for tier upgrades faster.
